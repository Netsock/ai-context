# Glossary

Status: Public reference

## Evidence
Material that supports a claim: source records, measurements, documents, quotations, logs, or other primary support.

## Observation
A recorded fact, event, field report, external signal, or primary input. An observation is not automatically a conclusion.

## Interpretation
An explanation, analysis, or meaning derived from evidence or observations. Interpretations remain revisable.

## Discussion
A preserved reasoning process containing hypotheses, comparisons, unresolved questions, alternative explanations, or exploratory analysis.

## Concept
A reusable abstraction used to express a recurring mechanism, structure, or current understanding.

## Knowledge
Reusable understanding intended to support future reasoning beyond one isolated event.

## Context
The currently relevant state, assumptions, constraints, relationships, and environment required to reason correctly.

## Current Context
Context that is presently active. It must be distinguished from historical information.

## Decision
An explicitly adopted policy, strategy, architecture, direction, or choice. A proposal or AI interpretation is not a Decision until adopted.

## Ontology
A structured representation of meaningful entities, relationships, rules, constraints, and concepts within a domain or organization.

In the SalesSeeds Knowledge OS context, company-specific ontology refers especially to the relationships and meaning structures that help explain how the organization interprets situations and makes decisions.

## RAG
Retrieval-Augmented Generation. An architecture in which relevant external knowledge is retrieved and supplied to a language model as context for generation or reasoning.

## Knowledge OS
A shared knowledge foundation for humans and AI that preserves company-specific context, knowledge structure, semantic status, authority, and traceability.

## Knowledge Nurturing
The iterative development of knowledge through observation, discussion, interpretation, human judgment, action, new evidence, and time.

## KAMOSU AI
The working name for the Knowledge Nurturing OS concept currently under development. It should not yet be treated as a finalized or fully validated product definition.

## Canonical source
The authoritative source whose content takes precedence when conflicting versions exist.

For this public repository, the canonical source is the separate private Knowledge OS.

## Public AI Context
A curated, public, AI-consumption layer distilled from the canonical private Knowledge OS.
