# KAMOSU AI

Status: Working concept under active development

KAMOSU AI is the working name for an AI Knowledge Nurturing OS being explored within SalesSeeds.

It should not yet be treated as a finalized product definition, proven methodology, or formally adopted market category.

## Core question

What makes an organization uniquely itself, and how can that meaning structure be discovered, tested, expressed, and made usable by AI?

## Working definition

KAMOSU AI is a human-AI knowledge nurturing system intended to help an organization continuously discover and develop company-specific context and ontology from real business activity.

The metaphor is "kamosu" — to ferment or brew.

Knowledge is not assumed to become valuable merely by being stored. It develops through repeated interaction among observations, discussion, interpretation, human judgment, action, and time.

## Knowledge Nurturing Loop

```text
Business activity
      ↓
Observation
      ↓
Discussion
      ↓
Interpretation / Hypothesis
      ↓
Concept / Knowledge
      ↓
Ontology / Context / Decision
      ↓
Action
      ↓
New Observation
```

The loop is iterative rather than one-way.

A concept may be revised when counterevidence appears. Exceptions may add conditions. Current context may replace historical assumptions. Results of actions return as new observations.

## Difference from RAG

RAG asks:

"Which existing information should be retrieved to answer this question?"

KAMOSU asks a broader question:

"What meaning structure makes this organization what it is, and how should that understanding evolve?"

RAG, vector databases, knowledge graphs, search, and other retrieval technologies may be implementation components of KAMOSU, but they are not the objective by themselves.

## Company-specific ontology

The working hypothesis is that important organizational knowledge exists not only in documents and databases but also in relationships such as:

- how decisions are actually made
- which exceptions matter
- what the organization refuses to do
- which signals experienced people notice
- why some customers, activities, or constraints matter more than others
- tacit rules developed through accumulated experience

KAMOSU aims to make these structures progressively more explicit and traceable without pretending that they can be designed perfectly in advance.

## Human-AI complementarity

Current working model:

AI strengths include memory, retrieval, organization, comparison, counterargument, recombination, and large-scale processing.

Human strengths include lived experience, embodiment, value judgment, discomfort or anomaly detection, intuition, meaning-making, and creative leaps.

KAMOSU is intended to circulate these different capabilities across time rather than simply replace human thinking with automated generation.

## Time as part of the system

A key hypothesis is that unfinished discussion can have value.

The system may deliberately support states such as:

- do not conclude yet
- preserve as discussion
- keep under nurturing
- revisit later
- reconnect with new observations or knowledge

If implemented successfully, "nurturing" becomes a system behavior rather than only a brand metaphor.

## Claims that are not yet established

Do not claim as fact that:

- KAMOSU measurably increases human creativity
- a specific nurturing interval causes better insight
- creativity can already be engineered reproducibly
- the current KAMOSU architecture is final

These remain hypotheses or future validation targets.
